One puff of steam venting from a short capped roof pipe. The pipe is a stub of dark dented metal at
the bottom, knee-high on a grown adult; the steam above it is a dense OPAQUE cloud painted as solid
blocks of colour with hard cel edges -- it is not translucent, not misty, not airbrushed, and nothing
behind it shows through it. The cloud is warm white and pale orange, rimmed hotter along the edge the
dusk sun catches from behind. It is never pink, never magenta and never purple.
The steam has only just started: a low stubby puff sitting right on the pipe's mouth, no taller than
the pipe itself. The whole plume stays well inside the canvas, with a wide band of magenta above it.

FRAMING:
One subject only, alone, painted over a completely flat #FF00FF magenta void. There is no ground, no
sky, no wall and no floor -- only the subject and the magenta. Do not paint scenery, a horizon, a
skyline, a rooftop, a building, or any second separate subject into the magenta. Nothing casts a
shadow onto the magenta.
Leave a clear band of magenta on all four sides: the subject must not touch or run off any edge of
the canvas.
The magenta is one single uniform colour from edge to edge of the canvas, with no gradient, no
texture, no noise and no light falling on it. Do not put a decorative border, a CRT bezel, a
vignette, scanlines or a matte around the canvas.
The camera stands at the eye level of a grown adult on the same rooftop and looks straight across at
the subject, neither down onto it nor up at it.
STYLE: 16-bit SNES arcade fighting-game stage art, hand-painted, chunky readable shapes, hard cel

outline drawn around the subject. A low dusk sun sits behind and to the left, so the subject is
mostly in warm shadow with a hot rim of light down its left edge.
